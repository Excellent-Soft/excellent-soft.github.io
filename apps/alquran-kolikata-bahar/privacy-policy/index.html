Privacy & Policy

Al-Quran Bangla (Kolkata Font) (also published as "Al Quran Bangla-Kolikata Font") is a Quran reading app published by Excellent Soft. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with your information. It applies to the Android and iOS versions of the app.

Data That We Collect

We do not collect personal data through the app. The app has no account or login, no purchases, no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools. We do not ask for, and the app does not send us, your name, email address, phone number, address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or any other personal information.

Data Stored On Your Device

The app keeps a few things only on your device so that it can remember where you stopped reading, such as the last surah you opened, and your display settings. This data is never uploaded to us. It is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

Internet Connection

Some features need an internet connection: the audio recitation is streamed from a content delivery network, and the Shan-e-Nuzul (context of revelation) text is loaded from our server at alquranbd.org. When your device makes these requests, the servers that deliver the content may keep standard technical logs, such as an IP address and the time of the request. We do not use these logs to identify you or to build a profile about you.

Sharing And Copying

If you choose to share or copy a verse, the text is passed to the app you select through your device's own sharing feature. We do not receive it.

Third Parties

We do not sell or pass any personal data to other companies, because we do not collect any. The app does not include advertising or analytics services from third parties.

Children

The app does not collect personal data from anyone, including children, and does not contain user-generated content or social features.

Security

Content is requested over secure (HTTPS) connections. Since the app does not collect personal data, there is no personal data on our servers to protect or disclose.

Changes To This Policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, for example if a feature that collects data is added, we will update this page before the new version is released.

Contact

If you have a question about this policy, please contact Excellent Soft using the developer contact details shown on the app's page in the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store.
