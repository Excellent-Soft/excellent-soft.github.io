Privacy & Policy

AWS & PC Health by Excellent Soft reports the health of your computer and your AWS account.

Data That We Collect

We do not collect, receive or store any of your data. The app reads device statistics (CPU, memory, disk, battery, uptime) and, if you configure AWS credentials, read-only AWS metadata (EC2 state, CloudWatch alarms, billing totals). This information is processed and shown on your device only.

AWS Credentials

The app uses the AWS credentials or profile already configured on your device and sends read-only requests directly from your device to AWS. We never see, copy or transmit your credentials, and they are not stored by the app.

Third Party Policy

We do not share data with third parties and do not use analytics or advertising. Requests you make to AWS are governed by the AWS Privacy Notice.

Security of Your Information

Because data stays on your device, its security depends on your device and AWS account security. We recommend using least-privilege, read-only IAM permissions.

Your Rights

Since we hold no personal data, there is nothing to access or delete. If you have questions about this policy, please contact Excellent Soft.
